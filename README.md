# sudoku-helper

A simple web-based tool to help you solve Sudoku puzzles on Discord.

## How to Use

1. **Copy and paste** the Sudoku puzzle from Discord into the text box.
2. Click **Load Puzzle** to display the Sudoku grid.
3. **Solve the puzzle!** You can enter numbers using the on-screen buttons or your keyboard's number pad.
4. Once you've finished, click **Create Answer** to generate your answer in the format required by the Discord bot.
5. Click **Copy Answer** to copy your answer to the clipboard.
6. **Paste your answer** into the answer field on Discord.

## Tips

- You don't need to copy only the puzzle grid. It's fine to include the surrounding text from the Discord message.
- Both the original text-based puzzle format and the newer emoji-based format are supported.
- You can use either the on-screen number buttons or your keyboard's number pad to fill in the cells.
